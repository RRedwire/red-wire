What we believe

Small surface.
Open source. Owned by you.

The most useful infrastructure is boring, inspectable and replaceable. We build tools that can be forked, deployed and shut down without asking anyone for permission.

Self-host first

Every product ships with a deployment path. If we disappeared tomorrow, your instance would keep running.

Security & transparency

What a relay
can and can't see.

Redline terminates Epoxy TLS at the destination. The relay forwards encrypted streams, so payloads stay encrypted in transit — but metadata is still metadata, and we won't pretend otherwise.

  • SeesDestination host, connection timing, byte volume and duration. Not page contents.
  • Doesn'tLog request bodies, credentials or browsing history by default.
  • DisclosureReport issues in the Discord or by email. We aim to acknowledge within 72 hours.
  • HardeningPin the lockfile, terminate TLS at the edge, rate-limit public relays, keep logs short.
